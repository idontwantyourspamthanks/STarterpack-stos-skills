# Sources (not redistributed)

The command entries in `commands/` were distilled from the official Control
extension documentation: CONTREG.DOC (V3.6b registered manual) and
CONTROL35.DOC (V3.5a shareware manual), by L.J. Greenhalgh (1994-96). The
verbatim manuals are not included for copyright reasons; they are widely
archived in the Atari community (the exxos STOS extensions archive carries
V3.0, V3.2, V3.5a and V3.6b packages, including the extension binaries and
example programs). The distilled entries in `commands/` and the notes in
`errata.md` carry their content.
